### What happened
Leads rose across the board and **every digital channel contributed**. The week was carried by search — Organic (48) and Paid Search (19). SALs more than doubled to 10, split evenly Organic 5 / Paid 5. Of the 74 MQLs, **6 already became SALs within their creation week** — a fast-conversion quality signal.

### Why
- **Organic — conversion, not traffic:** sessions were flat (~17.2K); the demo-request CTA converted better, concentrated on the homepage (22 of 38 demo fills).
- **Paid Search — efficiency, not spend:** spend stayed low (~$13K vs July's $40–51K) yet cost/MQL hit a period-best $743, concentrated on Brand + Compete terms.
- **One offer drove both:** Custom Video Demo Request ~2.5×'d across organic and paid at once, while Gated Free Trial and Talk to Sales slipped.

### Lead quality (Expected-Value model)
Average acquisition value trended up and held above $2K — a healthy sign. Best day was **9/23** (19 MQLs at the week's peak average and total acquisition value); it has already added $21K of value since creation with 3 of 19 converted to SAL.

### Implications
- Paid gains are efficiency-driven (durable) but sit in demand-capped Brand/Compete — scaling volume further needs nonbrand, currently starved.
- Volume over quality: the incremental mix skews to the demo CTA (~8% MQL→SAL) over Talk to Sales (~37%), so blended expected conversion sits near the norm (~10%).
